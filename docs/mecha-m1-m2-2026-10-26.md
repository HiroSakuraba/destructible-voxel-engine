# Mecha shooter — M1–M4 unified playable combat slice
Generated by `dve_create_mecha NEW_DIRECTORY`; run with `dve_player NEW_DIRECTORY`.
The scene uses XY horizontal, Z-up, and static polygon cover.

## Controls
- W/A/S/D (or gamepad left stick): move chassis independently of weapon yaw.
- Mouse motion or J/L (or gamepad right stick): turn turret; I/K choose stick aim vector.
- Left mouse: 18 HP autocannon every 0.105 s, 200 rounds.
- Right mouse: 37 m/s rockets, 0.85 s cooldown, 12 rockets, 128 projectile cap.
- Shift: 32-energy directional dash, energy regenerates at 16/s.
- P: pause. R: reset. F3: debug HUD.
- Kill all three visually outlined target machines to win.

The torso mesh is a separate visual object, and can rotate independently of the leg assembly.
A conservative swept capsule provides locomotion collision, with two slide iterations.
Targets are visual-only polygon meshes represented by gameplay spheres, so they receive
shots without unnecessary dynamic physics bodies. World geometry is resolved by capsule
sweep (voxel + polygon), with exact voxel ray checks also considered.

## M1/M2 boundaries
- No jumping, gravity-based falling, foot IK, recoil impulse or articulated legs yet.
- Mouse turning is relative, not absolute cursor-to-ground aiming.
- Weapon impacts can enqueue bounded voxel sphere damage for actual voxel obstacles,
  but the generated starter uses static polygon cover (M3 supplies destructible voxel cover).
- No enemy AI or mission objectives beyond target elimination (M4/M5).
- Full combat save/load intentionally remains an M5 task. Quickload rejects restoring
  combat-state gameplay rather than claiming success without restoring targets/ammunition.
- No independent GPU particle pipeline. M2 uses immediate target disable and basic HUD.
- A projectile's swept hit is authoritative: no end-point-only tunneling.
- The reference renderer in the standalone player remains CPU; GPU player backend is separate work.

## Validation
Compile `dve_mecha_game_tests` and run `ctest --test-dir build -R dve_mecha_game_tests --output-on-failure`.
The expected checks cover motor acceleration, energy accounting, invalid numeric controls,
scene validation, and deterministic combat test targets. Windowed visuals and high-load
performance must still be checked in a native environment.

## M3: geometry-driven destruction
- Two new anchored voxel barricades are authored into the exported Iron Yard map,
  and their physical voxels are directly hit by rockets.
- On impact, active voxels in a bounded radius are queued with
  `GameWorld::queue_damage_sphere`. Destruction is published by the normal
  GameWorld tick, so raycast, character collision and damage surface marks share
  one authoritative result.
- Autocannon impacts leave surface marks on voxels. A bounded pool of impact
  dust uses the existing CPU player world-space dust overlay (96 effect cap, aged
  and retired in fixed simulation time). This is deliberately not a new VFX backend.
- The debug HUD reports queued geometry carves, and the terrain's existing
  derived collision and optional fragment pipeline handles destruction.

## M4: three enemy archetypes
- `mech_target.ai_kind` 0 = stationary test dummy, 1 = scout, 2 = heavy
  walker, 3 = fixed turret. Existing M2 fixtures default to dummy.
- Enemy motion, sightlines, aim rates, damage, range and fire cadence are
  editable role properties; enemy hits reduce the player's health and may
  trigger a loss state. The camera and health HUD report victory/loss.
- Scout: closes at distance then strafes/orbits. Heavy: slower pursuit and
  retreats at very close range. Turret: holds position, turns and fires.
- An isolated Z-up **XY** A* tactical route planner (`mecha_navigation`) avoids
  the existing Y-up navmesh's coordinate mismatch. It caches sampled occupancy,
  forbids diagonal corner cutting, caps each query at 2,304 grid cells, and
  rate-limits replanning to six tenths of a second. Updated cover is
  re-evaluated without relying on stale navmesh tiles.
- All enemies use actual GameWorld ray queries for LOS. Enemy fire cannot
  cross intact polygon or voxel cover; destruction can create new firing lanes.

## Remaining limitations
- AI is intentionally local/tactical, not a global navigation mesh or squad
  coordination implementation. A full authored nav-mesh migration to Z-up is separate.
- Enemy weapons use LOS hitscan with cooldowns; missiles and telegraphs are future work.
- Environment edits persist across R wave restarts. Full scene reset including
  destructible cover and combat save/load require the M5 serialization work.
- The initial visual effects are CPU dust overlays, not GPU debris/particles.
- Third-person controls, jump jets, leg IK, and lock-on remain M6.

## Added M3/M4 verification
Run `ctest -R "dve_mecha_(game|m3_m4)_tests" --output-on-failure`.
The added suite covers authored voxel geometry, the three enemy role variants,
A* path correctness and boundedness, collision-protected LOS, enemy damage,
scout pursuit, navigation replanning, and an exported rocket breach.
