# F2 Wind Garden tutorial

Open a generated flying project and select the **Flight Wind Volume** markers in the scene. Adjust each marker's radius, height, strength and sink fraction.

- The yellow/orange marker at X 50, Y 280 corresponds to a training thermal. Circle within the core at a safe airspeed while watching the **VAR** reading.
- The second column near X 20, Y 520 supports the Bird stage. Switch to bird mode and use wings and rising air rather than exhausting flap stamina.
- The windward side of the eastern canyon ridge provides lift. Reverse aircraft `wind_x` and the rising side becomes the lee side.
- Enable **F3** for the full wind-vector and thermal/ridge readouts.

Glider mode remains **unpowered**. If the craft climbs without rising air or momentum converted from a dive, that is a physics bug. Hover mode consumes battery to maintain altitude in a crosswind. The vehicle keeps continuous momentum when morphing, including in moving air.
